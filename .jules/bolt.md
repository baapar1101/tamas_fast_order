## 2024-10-10 - Prevent unnecessary re-renders in Storefront
**Learning:** In the `StorefrontPage`, the global `cartLines` array (from `useCart`) is passed down to all `ProductCard` components. Since the cart state is updated immutably, a new `cartLines` array is created whenever *any* item is added to or updated in the cart. This causes every single `ProductCard` on the page to fail `React.memo`'s default shallow prop comparison, resulting in dozens or hundreds of unnecessary re-renders across the page on every cart interaction.
**Action:** Always provide a custom `propsAreEqual` function to `React.memo` when a component receives a large, frequently updated array/object prop (like a cart state or global configuration) where only a small subset of the data is relevant to that specific component. The custom comparator should perform a deep check only on the data subset (e.g. quantities of specific variants) required by the component.
