## 2024-11-04 - Quantity Stepper ARIA Labels
**Learning:** In this codebase, visual-only interactive elements like quantity stepper buttons (`+`/`-`) often lack descriptive `aria-label`s, making them inaccessible to screen readers.
**Action:** When working on UI components, specifically look for icon-only or visual-only buttons (like `+`/`-`) and ensure they have localized (Persian) `aria-label` attributes to properly convey their functionality to all users.
