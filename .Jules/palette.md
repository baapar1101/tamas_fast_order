## 2026-11-05 - Add ARIA labels to quantity stepper buttons
**Learning:** Textual but visually symbolic buttons like "+" and "-" used for quantity steppers (e.g., `.add-wh-btn`) often rely on visual context or `title` attributes, lacking proper `aria-label` attributes for screen readers.
**Action:** Always ensure that icon-only or symbolic buttons (like +/-) include explicit, descriptive `aria-label` attributes in Persian (e.g., "افزایش" and "کاهش") for better accessibility.
