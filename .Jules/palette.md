## 2024-10-09 - Accessible Theme Toggle\n**Learning:** The frontend makes extensive use of the `Icon` component within buttons, many of which lack proper `aria-label` attributes despite having a `title`. This creates a gap for screen reader users where hover text exists but programmatic identification is missing.\n**Action:** Proactively search for `icon-only` or `btn-icon-only` classes on `button` or `Link` elements to verify and add `aria-label` alongside the `title` attribute.
